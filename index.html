AboutA career split between industry and academia

Currently leads Agile delivery of a multi-tenant SaaS transformation as Engineering Team Lead at the Center for Internet Security, working daily with Product, QA, and Engineering to refine the backlog, write acceptance criteria, estimate work, and collaborate directly with customers.

Teaching has run alongside that industry work throughout her career. She currently teaches in the Department of Cybersecurity at the University at Albany, bringing current, hands-on industry practice into the classroom. Before that, she spent over a decade as a tenured professor in Siena University’s Computer Science / Information Systems department, teaching software engineering, mobile app development, and web design, and chaired the department. Earlier in her career, she led enterprise ERP implementations and legacy data conversions at the University at Albany, balancing competing priorities across many stakeholders as a technical team lead.

She holds a Ph.D. in Informatics, an MBA in Management Information Systems, a Graduate Certificate in Cybersecurity, and a B.S. in Computer Science, and is a Certified Scrum Master and AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner.
